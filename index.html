HOW TO PLAY

DESKTOP CONTROLS

  • W / S: Pitch Forward / Back
  • A / D: Roll Left / Right
  • SPACE / SHIFT: Thrust Up / Down
  • Q / E: Yaw (Rotate) Left / Right
  • R: Reset Drone

MOBILE CONTROLS

  • Left Stick: Drag UP for Altitude (Climb), DOWN to Descend, LEFT/RIGHT to Rotate (Yaw).
  • Right Stick: Drag to tilt Forward, Backward, Left, or Right to move in that direction.
  • Auto-Hover: Keeps altitude steady automatically.
  • RESET: Button at top of HUD if you get lost.
